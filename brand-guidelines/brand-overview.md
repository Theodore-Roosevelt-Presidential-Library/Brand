# TRPL Brand Overview

## Who We Are

The Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library is a presidential library dedicated to Theodore Roosevelt's enduring legacy — located in Medora, North Dakota, the landscape that shaped him. We open July 4, 2026, timed to America's 250th anniversary.

We are not a museum that preserves the past under glass. We are an institution that uses the past to inspire action in the present. TR's stories of conservation, civic duty, and the strenuous life are not relics — they are instructions for living.

## Mission & Purpose

The Library exists to:
- Preserve and share TR's life, legacy, and ideas
- Inspire civic engagement and leadership across a politically diverse American audience
- Connect visitors to the Badlands landscape that transformed Roosevelt
- Serve as a gathering place for ideas, scholarship, and civic life

## Brand Voice

**Action-oriented. Decisive. Inspiring. Earnest.**

Think of a knowledgeable park ranger who is genuinely excited to share what they know — not a professor behind a podium. We are intellectually serious without being inaccessible. Warm without being casual. Authoritative without being stuffy.

We lead with story, not institution. TR's life is inherently dramatic and compelling. We use narrative, anecdote, and vivid detail to draw people in before delivering institutional information.

**We are not:** ad-copy clever, ironic, sentimental, partisan, or vague.

## Content Hierarchy

Every piece of TRPL content should move through this arc:

**Story → Connection → Visit**

1. **Story** — A specific, concrete moment from TR's life or legacy
2. **Connection** — What that moment means for us today
3. **Visit** — An invitation to experience it in Medora

## Audience

Our content speaks to a broad, politically diverse American audience. TR's legacy — conservation, leadership, civic duty, the strenuous life — touches values shared across the spectrum. We never position TR's legacy as belonging to one political side.

**Primary audiences:**
- General visitors and families planning trips to Medora
- Educators and students
- Donors and prospective benefactors
- Corporate and nonprofit partners
- Media and journalists
- Government officials
- The Roosevelt scholarly community

## The Badlands as Brand Asset

Our location in western North Dakota is both a logistical challenge and our greatest storytelling asset. Roosevelt's transformative experience in the Badlands — coming here after the deaths of his mother and first wife, rebuilding himself through hard physical labor and the landscape — is central to our narrative. We lean into the remoteness, the scale, the sense of place. We do not apologize for the drive.

## Institutional Terminology (Non-Negotiable)

| Use | Never Use |
|---|---|
| participants | visitors, guests |
| Trustees | board members, board of directors |
| benefactors | donors |
| the Library | the library (uncapitalized) |
| the Foundation | the foundation (uncapitalized) |
| T.R. (with periods) | TR (without periods) in formal contexts |

**"Teddy"** is never used in prose. The one exception is the hashtag #TeddyRoosevelt for search discovery.

## TR Quotes

TR's words must be quoted verbatim. Never paraphrase, modernize, or alter a quote. If the exact wording cannot be confirmed from the approved quotes spreadsheet (TR_Center__TR_Quotes.xlsx, "Likely Verified – Has Title & Date" filter), do not use it as a direct quote — describe the idea he expressed instead.

Always cite the source: speech title, letter recipient, book title, and date when available.

Misattributed or modified quotes are a serious reputational risk for a presidential library.
