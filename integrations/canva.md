# Canva

Library designs are built in Canva with the Library's brand kit, so they carry
the real brand fonts and colors. Ask Marketing & Communications to add you to
the Library's Canva team and brand kit.

## The brand kit should hold

- **Colors**, grouped as in the guide: Background (Dark Forest `#1B4532`, Night
  Sky `#092A4D`, Dark Gray `#25282A`, White), Grounding (Sand `#D1CCBD`, Deep
  Orange `#E7805D`, Bright Forest `#8FC895`, Gray Sky `#99ADC5`), Accent (Sunset
  Pink `#F36079`, Sunset Orange `#FC924E`, Sunset Yellow `#F9D635`, Spring Green
  `#87BB41`).
- **Fonts:** Dharma Gothic E (headings, set to uppercase), ITC Clearface
  (subheadings and body), Frutiger Next (small text and captions). Teddy
  Regular only for approved uses.
- **Logos and marks:** the SVG files from [Logos and marks](../assets/README.md).
- **Photos:** real photographs of the Library and the Badlands. No renderings.

## Brand voice for Canva's AI writing

Paste into the brand kit's **Brand voice** field:

> Friendly, plainspoken and specific, like a park ranger excited to share what
> they know. Lead with story and place, not the institution. Short sentences,
> active voice, concrete detail; no "world-class" or "transformative." Say
> "participants" (not visitors), "Trustees," "Benefactors," "T.R." (never
> "Teddy"), "buttes" (not mesas). Nonpartisan. Never invent or paraphrase a
> Theodore Roosevelt quote.

Canva's AI does not verify history. Every historical claim and quote still goes
through the accuracy check before publishing.

## Templates

Start from the Library's templates rather than a blank page. For decks, use the
master presentation template. Photos with text over them use a Night Sky
gradient behind the text.

## With Claude and the Canva connector

If Claude has the Canva connector, ask it to build or revise designs directly:
"Make an Instagram post for ... in Canva." The `design-brief` skill picks the
template, applies the spec and leaves the design for you to review. Then ask
"Review this design" to run the design review.

## Before you export

Run through [Visual identity](../plugins/trpl-brand/guide/visual-identity.md):
one background color, two color families, contrast on every piece of text,
logo clear space, Dharma in caps, no hyphenation.
