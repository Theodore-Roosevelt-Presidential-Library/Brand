# Brand assets

Logos, supporting marks and fonts for the Theodore Roosevelt Presidential
Library. Read the [visual identity rules](../plugins/trpl-brand/guide/visual-identity.md)
before using any of them. Never recolor, redraw or rearrange a logo; use the
colorway file you need, or ask for one.

Formats: **SVG** for web and Canva, **PNG** (transparent) for office documents
and quick use, **EPS** (CMYK) for professional print. Supporting marks may be
recolored in approved brand colors; logos may not.

## Logos

| Logo | When | Files |
|------|------|-------|
| **Stacked (primary)** | First choice whenever space allows | [`logos/stacked/`](logos/stacked/): black, dark gray, white |
| **Horizontal** | Only when stacked doesn't fit: website headers, sponsor placements | [`logos/horizontal/`](logos/horizontal/): black |
| **Boxed lockup** | Busy backgrounds, stickers, patches, labels | [`logos/boxed/`](logos/boxed/): dark gray on white, black on white, white on dark gray |

On white, prefer **dark gray** over black. On Dark Gray, Dark Forest or Night
Sky, use **white**.

Clear space: two "T" widths on every side (not built into the files). Minimum
width: 0.85 in stacked, 1.25 in horizontal.

## Supporting marks

| Mark | Files |
|------|-------|
| **TR Brand Mark** (TR ringed by the institution's name) | [`marks/tr-brand/`](marks/tr-brand/): black, dark gray, white, Deep Orange |
| **TR Values Mark** (TR ringed by the four Values) | [`marks/tr-values/`](marks/tr-values/): black, dark gray, white, Sunset Yellow |
| **Elkhorn Mark** (T.R.'s Elkhorn Ranch brand ringed by the Values) | [`marks/elkhorn/`](marks/elkhorn/): black, dark gray, white, Gray Sky, Spring Green, Sunset Orange, Sunset Pink |

Marks, unlike logos, may be recolored in any approved brand color with enough
contrast. The colorways here are set to the exact brand hex values. EPS files
are the designer's CMYK originals; SVG and PNG colorways were made from the
black originals.

Minimum size 0.75 in, measured on the visible mark. Some EPS files carry extra
padding; size to the circle, not the file.
