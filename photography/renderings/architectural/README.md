# Architectural Renderings

Architectural renderings of the Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library building designed by **Snøhetta** (Oslo/New York). Photography/rendering credit: **Plomp** (unless otherwise noted on specific files).

## Subfolders

### `exterior/`
Press-appropriate exterior views of the main building: aerial, entry plaza, breezeway, dawn/dusk, elysium views, and rooftop. These are the primary renders used in press and marketing materials.

### `interior/`
Press-appropriate interior spaces: lobby, café, auditorium, trailhead, boardroom, museum store, narrative gallery, temporary gallery, admin spaces, classroom, and healing moment gallery. Confirm intended use before publishing individual renders.

### `site-and-landscape/`
Site-level renders showing the Library's relationship to the Badlands landscape: overlook views, eco-final site plan, sunken garden, berm, plaza, parking approach, and M&O building.

### `internal-use-only/`
Architectural drawings, floor plans, section drawings, material boards, and study drawings. For internal team use only. Do not publish or distribute externally.

## About the Building

The TRPL building was designed by **Snøhetta**, selected after an international competition in August 2020 that included finalists Studio Gang and Henning Larsen. Snøhetta's design is informed by Roosevelt's personal reflections on the landscape, his interest in environmental stewardship, and his periods of quiet introspection.

The main building's earthen roof rises from the butte, echoing the landforms of the surrounding Badlands. Participants can ascend the accessible rooftop to discover commanding views of Theodore Roosevelt National Park and Medora. Inside, large windows highlight views to historically significant landscapes. A generous covered breezeway connects the two lobbies and can function as one open-air space in good weather.

The Library opens **July 4, 2026**.
