# TRPL Photography Style Guide

## Visual Direction

TRPL photography is grounded, authentic, and specific to place. Every image should feel like it could only exist in western North Dakota or in connection to Theodore Roosevelt's specific story.

**Core qualities:**
- **Authentic** — real people, real moments, real landscape. Not posed or stock.
- **Specific** — images that couldn't belong to any other institution
- **Grounded** — the Badlands light, scale, and texture inform the palette
- **Earnest** — we don't do irony or art-school cool

## Subject Categories

### The Badlands Landscape
The landscape is the Library's most powerful visual asset. Prioritize:
- Wide, expansive shots that communicate scale and remoteness
- Golden hour and dramatic weather conditions
- The specific landforms TR would have known: buttes, coulees, river breaks
- Seasonal variety (winter isolation, spring wildflowers, summer storms, fall color)

### The Building
Designed by Snøhetta — architecturally distinctive. Prioritize:
- Exterior shots that show the building in relationship to the landscape
- Interior shots that connect architecture to TR's story
- Details: materials, craft, sustainability features

### People / Participants
- Authentic engagement — people learning, discovering, reacting
- Diverse in age, background, and relationship to TR's story
- Staff and experts shown in context, not posed for the camera
- Children experiencing the exhibits

### Theodore Roosevelt (Historical)
- Source from approved archives: Library of Congress, Harvard TR Collection, Theodore Roosevelt Center at Dickinson State
- Always credit with full collection information
- Prefer images that show TR in action — not formal portraits
- Verify usage rights before publication

## Style Parameters

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Color treatment | Natural, minimal post-processing; warm but not oversaturated |
| Filters | None that change the fundamental color character |
| Cropping | Respect compositional integrity; don't crop to eliminate context |
| Text overlays | Only using approved brand typography and sufficient contrast |
| Watermarks | Do not watermark TRPL-owned photography |

## Image Specifications

| Use | Minimum Resolution | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Web / digital | 72dpi at display size | JPG (80%+ quality) |
| Print | 300dpi at print size | JPG or TIFF |
| Social media | Platform-native dimensions | JPG or PNG |
| Email | 72dpi, max 1200px wide | JPG |

## Rights & Credits

All photography in the `examples/` folder must have a corresponding rights/credit note. Use this format in the file name or a companion `.md`:

**Owned by TRPL:** No credit required in materials; document photographer and date internally.
**Licensed:** Document license type, expiration, and permitted uses.
**Archival (public domain):** Credit as: "Courtesy [Collection Name], [Institution]"
