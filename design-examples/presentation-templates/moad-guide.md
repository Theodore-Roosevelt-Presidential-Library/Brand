# MOAD — Mother of All Decks

`moad.pdf` is TRPL's master presentation. It contains every slide we use, organized into modular sections. When building a presentation for a donor, partner, government official, or media contact, you pull the relevant sections from this deck and arrange them for that audience. You never start from scratch — you start from the MOAD.

## Structure

The deck is 114 pages organized into 7 named sections plus a guide page. Section dividers use the format `S E C T I O N  N A M E` in spaced all-caps — they're visual chapter markers, not content slides, and are typically removed in final presentations.

| Section | Pages | Use When |
|---|---|---|
| **Custom Slides** | 2–7 | Donor/partner-specific proposals — priority overlap, gift breakdown, blended proposals |
| **Overview & Location** | 8–45 | Orienting any new audience — who we are, where we are, why it matters, timeline, press |
| **Experience Slides** | 46–67 | Visitor-facing story — what people will do, see, and feel; talent; comparable museums |
| **Sustainability Slides** | 68–97 | Conservation mission, Living Building Challenge, Four Zeros, certifications |
| **Curriculum Slides** | 98–101 | Education program, K-12 outreach, TR Scholars Program |
| **Construction Slides** | 102–109 | Building progress — use for construction updates, near-completion milestones |
| **Closing Slides** | 110–114 | Thank-you, call to action, trlibrary.com |

## Slide Types

### Cover Slide
**Format:** "Prepared with Gratitude for [COMPANY/INDIVIDUAL]" + month/year

Personalization is baked in from slide 1. Always update the recipient name and date. The tone — "with Gratitude" — sets a warm, relational register that runs through the whole deck.

### Section Divider
**Format:** Centered, spaced all-caps text (`E X P E R I E N C E  S L I D E S`)

These are internal navigation markers. They're useful when presenting the full deck internally but should usually be removed in external presentations. They tell you which section you're in, not the audience.

### Quote Slide
**Format:** Full-page TR quote in large type, attribution in em dash + name

TR quotes are used structurally — not decoratively. Each section opens with or is anchored by a relevant Roosevelt quote that frames the content that follows. These are never paraphrased or modified. See the source notes below for verified quotes used in the MOAD.

**Examples from the deck:**
- *"I never would have been President if it had not been for my experiences in North Dakota."* (Overview section)
- *"Of all the questions which can come before this nation…there is none which compares in importance with the great central task of leaving this land even better for our descendants than it is for us."* (Sustainability section — used twice)
- *"We must dare to be great; and we must realize that greatness is the fruit of toil and sacrifice and high courage."* (Closing section)

### Comparison Table
**Format:** Two-column or three-column contrast layout

Used to position TRPL against the familiar (other presidential libraries, other institutions) to show differentiation. The core comparison in the deck:

| Other Presidential Libraries Have | TRPL Has |
|---|---|
| Guests | Participants |
| Exhibits | Experiences |
| President's Story | People's Story |
| First Draft of History | Legacy for Tomorrow |
| Archives | A Digital Platform |
| Static Museum | Living Library |

### Data/Statistics Slide
**Format:** Large number or stat as anchor, supporting context in body text

Used for conservation facts (230 million acres protected, 150 national forests, 5 national parks), attendance projections (800,000 THRO visitors, 26,000 K-12 students annually), and facility specs (100+ acres prairie restoration, 400,000 native plants, 216 geothermal wells).

### Gallery/Program Overview Slide
**Format:** Bold headline + 3-column icon/text grid or bullet list

Used to introduce program categories (Adventure Galleries, Trailblazer Technology, Nature Moments; or the Four Zeros: Water, Carbon, Energy, Waste). The grid format keeps parity between parallel elements.

### People/Talent Slide
**Format:** Name + title/description, sometimes with photo

Used to highlight key participants (Nick Offerman as Voice of the Museum, Michael Chernus as TR, In the Arena voice talent). The talent roster for In the Arena as of the MOAD:
Ken Burns, Garth Brooks, Van Jones, George W. Bush, Doris Kearns Goodwin, Mitt Romney, Tom Selleck, Jon Batiste, Margaret Hoover, John Avlon, Ryan Holiday

### Timeline Slide
**Format:** Horizontal year progression with milestone labels

The MOAD uses a 2020–2030 timeline. Key dates: land acquired, architectural competition won by Snøhetta, groundbreaking, $100M raised, grand opening July 4, 2026, carbon neutrality milestone, ND state $50M endowment unlocked.

### Press Highlights Slide
**Format:** Multiple variants exist in the deck (pages 39–45)

Several layout versions showing the same three accolades (NYT 52 Places to Go in 2026, Lonely Planet Best in Travel 2026, Condé Nast Best of the World 2026). Use the layout version that fits your slide count and presentation format. The hero statement: *"TRPL isn't just a place, it's a pilgrimage."*

### Custom/Proposal Slides
**Format:** Donor-specific with `TKTKTK` or `[COMPANY/INDIVIDUAL]` placeholder text

Pages 3–7 are templates for building bespoke asks. Includes:
- **Priority overlap** — maps the donor's known priorities against TRPL's focus areas
- **TKTKTK Gift to America** — potential gift breakdown at the naming/lead level ($250M example: $100M Living Building, $100M TR Scholars, $50M Conservation Endowment)
- **Blended proposal** — combines TRPL and National Park Foundation asks into a single gift opportunity

### Closing Slide
**Format:** TR quote + gratitude message + trlibrary.com

Two closing slide variants in the deck — one shorter, one with a longer quote. Both end on the same note: we're building something for the generations. The standard CTA is `trlibrary.com`.

## Design Conventions

**Slide titles** use letter-spaced all-caps: `T H E  L I V I N G  B U I L D I N G  C H A L L E N G E`. This is a consistent typographic signature across the whole deck — it signals a TRPL slide.

**Body text** is concise. Paragraphs rarely exceed 4–5 lines. The MOAD favors short, declarative sentences over long explanations.

**Multiple variants of key slides** exist throughout (see press highlights, grand opening, and drone show pages). The MOAD is a library, not a linear presentation. When building a deck, you choose the variant that fits your layout needs.

**Lorem ipsum appears** on a few slides (pages 36–38) — the commemorative locomotive and drone show slides. These are placeholders awaiting final copy.

**Seasonal operating hours** (page 49) are embedded in the Experience section. Always verify against current schedule before including in an external presentation.

## Key Messaging Frameworks

**The Three Values:** Leadership, Conservation, Civic Engagement — TR's core legacy, and the Library's organizing mission. Used throughout as an anchor triplet.

**The TR Triangle:** TRPL + Mount Rushmore + Yellowstone National Park. Frames the Library as part of a regional pilgrimage circuit centered on the 100th meridian.

**The America Timeline:** 1776 (founding) → 1901 (TR's first year as president, 125 years later) → 2026 (TRPL opening, 250 years after founding) → 2276 (America at 500). Positions the Library at the exact midpoint of American history.

**Come In to Get Out:** The Library's interior draws visitors into the landscape, not away from it. The visit is a gateway to the Badlands, not a substitute for it.

**The Four Zeros:** Zero Water, Zero Carbon, Zero Energy, Zero Waste — the Living Building Challenge imperatives that define TRPL's sustainability ambition.

**Reinventing the Presidential Library:** TRPL is explicitly positioned against the traditional presidential library model. Participants, not guests. Experiences, not exhibits. Living library, not static museum.

## Notes for AI-Assisted Presentation Work

- Never alter TR quotes. If a quote isn't in this deck or in the key references, flag it for verification before use.
- The placeholder `TKTKTK` marks fields requiring donor/partner-specific research before a presentation is finalized.
- Operating hours, ticket pricing, and membership benefits change seasonally — always verify before including.
- The comparison table (TRPL vs. other presidential libraries) is a core positioning tool. Use it in any presentation where the audience may be unfamiliar with what makes TRPL different.
- The In the Arena talent roster is public but sensitive — confirm current participation before including in any external document.
