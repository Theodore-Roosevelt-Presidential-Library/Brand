# Find T.R. images and film

Read `guide/imagery-and-media.md` at the plugin root. It lists the sources and
the rules.

## Procedure

1. **Pin down the need:** subject, period, mood, format (still or moving),
   orientation and size, and where it will be used.
2. **Library-owned first.** If the person is staff or an approved partner,
   point them to the Library's digital asset library (Acquia DAM) and its film
   collection; anything there is cleared for Library use. Access is through
   Marketing & Communications. Don't guess at internal links.
3. **Public collections next**, in this order for T.R.:
   Library of Congress (Prints & Photographs; the Theodore Roosevelt Papers;
   *Theodore Roosevelt: His Life and Times on Film*; audio) → Harvard's
   Theodore Roosevelt Collection → Theodore Roosevelt Center Digital Library →
   NPS sites → National Archives → NYPL → Smithsonian. Search the collection
   directly; give the person search links and specific record links when you
   find them.
4. **For each candidate**, report:
   - the holding institution's record link
   - date, place, people and creator **as the record states them** (quote the
     record; don't fill gaps)
   - rights statement as published, and the credit line it asks for
   - resolution available
5. **Captions:** draft captions only from the record. Mark anything the record
   doesn't state as unknown. Send the caption through
   `trpl-brand:historian-handoff` before publishing (identifications, dates and
   "who is pictured" are claims).
6. **Film:** use the highest-resolution copy; remove every archive-made title
   card and later restoration card; split multi-event reels into clips A, B, C
   in reel order, each citing the parent reel; credit TRA material to the TRA;
   check whether Spanish-American War footage is staged.

## Never

- Use a Pinterest, Wikipedia or stock-site copy without finding the original record.
- Crop out a credit or watermark from an image without rights.
- Generate an AI image of T.R., any historical person, the Library or any
  identifiable building, or present an AI image as a photograph.
- Use the Library's architectural renderings.

## Contemporary imagery

Badlands, river, sky, wildlife and the Library: Library asset library first;
NPS imagery of Theodore Roosevelt National Park (public domain when NPS-made;
check each record) second. AI imagery only for landscape or wildlife, never
buildings, with "no buildings, no architecture, no monuments, no structures of
any kind" in the prompt.
