# TRPL Typography

Source: *Brand Identity System & Guidelines* (Local Projects, May 27, 2020)

## Typography Philosophy

TRPL's type system reflects the duality of Theodore Roosevelt's character — boldness and empathy, action and reflection. Dharma Gothic drives the calls to action with authority. ITC Clearface brings warmth and depth to the body. Frutiger Next handles the utilitarian supporting work with clarity.

---

## The Three-Font System

### 1. Dharma Gothic E — Display & Headlines

> "Dharma Gothic is an antiqued sans serif inspired by 1800s-style wood type." — Brand Guidelines

- **Use:** Display font only. Calls to action, headlines, titling.
- **Case:** Always all-caps
- **Weights in use:** ExBold (primary), Bold (secondary)
- **Style:** Conveys boldness, authority, a touch of the historical
- **Files:** `/assets/fonts/dharma-gothic/`

### 2. ITC Clearface — Body Copy

> "ITC Clearface is used for the secondary typeface, and should be used for body copy." — Brand Guidelines

- **Use:** All body copy and long-form text
- **Designer:** Victor Caruso, 1979 (redesign of Morris Fuller Benton's 1907 Clearface)
- **Character:** Warm, curving design influenced by the Arts and Crafts movement
- **Size (print):** 11pt standard (per letterhead spec in brand guidelines)
- **Size (web):** 16–18px base
- **Line height:** 1.5–1.65
- **Available weights:** Regular, Italic, Bold, BoldItalic, Heavy, HeavyItalic, Black, BlackItalic
- **Files:** `/assets/fonts/clearface/`

### 3. Frutiger Next Pro — Captions & Small Text

> "Frutiger Next should be used for captions, credits or the like, and only at scales 10pt or smaller." — Brand Guidelines

- **Use:** Captions, credits, legal/fine print, supporting labels
- **Maximum size:** 10pt (print) — this font is for small-scale use only
- **Designer:** Adrian Frutiger, 1997 update to Frutiger
- **Character:** Humanist sans-serif, highly legible at a distance or small sizes; tall, slightly condensed letterforms
- **Available weights:** Light, LightItalic, Regular, Italic, Bold, BoldItalic
- **Files:** `/assets/fonts/frutiger-next/`

### Bonus: Teddy-Regular — Decorative

- **Use:** Decorative/accent use only; not for body copy or headlines
- **Files:** `/assets/fonts/teddy/`

---

## Usage Rules

- **Dharma Gothic** is display only — all caps, never for body text
- **ITC Clearface** is the body copy standard — do not substitute
- **Frutiger Next** is reserved for 10pt and smaller — do not use at large sizes
- Never stretch or condense type horizontally or vertically
- Never use Dharma Gothic in mixed case — always all caps
- Italic is used for book/publication titles, foreign terms, and emphasis — not for decoration
