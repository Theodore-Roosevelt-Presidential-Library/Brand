# Theodore Roosevelt essentials

Orientation for writers and designers new to the subject. **This page is a
starting point, not a source.** Every historical claim in published work is
checked independently; see [Accuracy and approvals](accuracy-and-approvals.md).

## Names and titles

- **T.R.** (with periods) or **Theodore Roosevelt**. Never "Teddy" in prose.
- Period-correct titles: **Governor Roosevelt** (New York), **President
  Roosevelt**, and **Colonel Roosevelt** after the presidency, following the
  Story Guide's conventions.

## Why the Badlands matter to the Library

The Library stands in the country where Roosevelt ranched in the 1880s. The
Badlands years are central to the Library's story: grief, hard work, the
landscape, and the beginnings of his conservation thinking. Tell that story
through verified detail, not legend. Many Badlands anecdotes come from later
reminiscences; HistorianPanel has specialists who judge how reliable each one is.

## The pillars in his life

- **Leadership:** stepping forward and taking responsibility.
- **Citizenship:** his call to be "in the arena." (Quote it only from a verified
  text, with its source; see [Quotations](editorial-style-guide.md#quotations).)
- **Conservation:** national forests, parks, monuments, bird and game
  reservations. Every number and designation is a sourced claim.

## The hard chapters

The Library's tenet is **humanize, don't lionize.** Roosevelt's record includes
race and civil rights, Indigenous peoples and land, empire and war, immigration,
and eugenics-era views. Do not avoid these subjects, and do not take sides in
present-day politics about them.

- Describe what happened and what he said, accurately and with sources.
- Present real scholarly disagreement as disagreement.
- Content about Indigenous peoples is always reviewed before publishing.

## Traps that catch new writers

| Trap | Instead |
|------|---------|
| "Teddy" | T.R. |
| "San Juan Hill" | Kettle Hill; the San Juan Heights |
| "mesa" | butte |
| A great quote from a website or a meme | Check the [misquotes pages](https://www.trlibrary.com/tr/quotes/misquotes) and verify the exact wording at its source |
| The "never would have been President without North Dakota" line | Don't use it (wording unverified) |
| Offices, ranks and careers of his children and grandchildren | Verify each one; family members are often credited with offices they never held |
| "Before him" / "succeeded him" sequences | Verify the sequence; it is easy to skip an officeholder |
| Generations in the family tree (great-grandson versus great-great-grandson) | Verify the chain one generation at a time |
| Photo captions copied from the web | Match the holding institution's catalog record |
| "First," "only," "youngest" | Source it or cut it |
| Conservation acreage and counts | Source each figure; totals vary by how they are counted |
