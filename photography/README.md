# Photography

Photography direction and representative image examples for TRPL communications.

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| [style-guide.md](./style-guide.md) | Photography art direction, style principles, sourcing guidelines |
| `examples/` | Representative approved photos showing the brand's visual language |

## Naming Convention for Examples

`trpl_photo_[subject]_[orientation]_[source-or-date].[ext]`

Examples:
- `trpl_photo_badlands-landscape_horizontal_2025.jpg`
- `trpl_photo_building-exterior_vertical_snohetta-2025.jpg`
- `trpl_photo_tr-archival_portrait_loc-collection.jpg`

## Sourcing Notes

- Original Badlands/Medora photography: credit and rights info must be documented
- TR archival photography: source from Library of Congress or Harvard TR Collection; always credit
- Building/construction photography: coordinate with Tony Erickson and JE Dunn for approved images
- Do not upload images without confirmed rights clearance
