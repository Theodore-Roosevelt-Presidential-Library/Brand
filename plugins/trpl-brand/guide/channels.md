# Channels

The [voice](voice-and-tone.md) and the [editorial style guide](editorial-style-guide.md)
apply everywhere. These are the extra rules for each channel.

## Website (trlibrary.com)

- **Every page has an SEO title, a meta description and an Open Graph image**
  (a real photograph, with alt text).
- **Hours live only at** [/visit/hours](https://www.trlibrary.com/visit/hours). Link; don't restate.
- No current or future years in evergreen visitor copy. Write seasons as months.
- A visit is about three and a half hours. Never suggest less is typical.
- Introduce links with a title and a short teaser for each, not a count.
- Write for AI search as well as people: answer the question in the first
  sentence, use specific names and places, and keep facts consistent with the
  rest of the site.

## Social media

- **Every post carries a way to visit** (trlibrary.com/visit or a ticket link),
  except press-coverage posts.
- Long-form set: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X. Short-form (under 300
  characters): Threads, Bluesky.
- **Hashtags:** #DareGreatly (evergreen), #TR250 (campaign). #TeddyRoosevelt is
  allowed as a hashtag only.
- Verify every handle before tagging.
- **Press-coverage posts:**
  - Tag the outlet and author wherever the platform allows.
  - Link **only** to the article. A second link breaks automatic article previews.
  - Instagram: include the article link in the caption, and use the article's
    photos with a credit line (`Photos: Name`).
  - Threads doesn't pull preview images; attach the photo.
- On This Day (#OTD) posts follow the accuracy rule like everything else.

## Email

- From name: **Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library**, always.
- One primary call to action.
- Plain, specific subject lines.

## Press

- Lead with the news. Put the most important fact first.
- Never lead with a negative.
- Thank sponsors and Benefactors once, in a sentence or a standard paragraph.
- Quotes from Library leadership are approved by the person quoted.
- See [Accuracy and approvals](accuracy-and-approvals.md#what-needs-review)
  for what needs review.

## Letters from leadership

- Composed and formal; never folksy or familiar.
- Gratitude said once, plainly and specifically. See [Gratitude](editorial-style-guide.md#gratitude).
- Direct and factual about vision. Skip flowery language.

## Presentations

- Start from the Library's master presentation template in Canva (request
  access from Marketing & Communications).
- Covers use the real logo.
- Real photographs, not renderings. For text over photos, use a Night Sky
  gradient behind the text.

## Design work

- Build Library designs in **Canva** with the Library's brand kit (fonts and
  colors), unless Marketing & Communications agrees otherwise.
- Use real photographs from the Library's asset library. See [Imagery and media](imagery-and-media.md).
- Follow [Visual identity](visual-identity.md) for logo, color and type.

## Print, merchandise and signage

- Use Pantone C values for spot color and embroidery, Pantone U on uncoated stock.
- Wayfinding, donor recognition and environmental signage headlines use **TRPL
  Gothic**, matched to existing signage mechanicals.
- Patterns are for retail and apparel only, and drawn from authentic sources.
