# TRPL Color System

Source: *Brand Identity System & Guidelines* (Local Projects, May 27, 2020)

## Color Philosophy

Theodore Roosevelt cared deeply for nature. The palette borrows from the most profound and beautiful moments witnessed in the Badlands of North Dakota — vibrant and organic, it can be extended to include additional accent colors at the right proportions.

## Usage Rules

- **Dark Gray** (`#25282A`) is the primary brand color — it appears in the wordmark and sets the institutional tone
- **Sand** is the go-to warm background for print; White for most digital
- **Accent colors** are used for headline text only — never as large background fields or body copy
- The palette can be extended with additional accent colors at the right proportions, staying within the same Badlands-inspired temperature and saturation range
- Envelope paper and notecard envelopes may be selected to match **Deep Orange** per the original brand guidelines
- Business card stock colors referenced in the guidelines: Sand, Deep Orange, Bright Forest, and Gray Sky

## Accessibility

When using accent colors for text, verify contrast ratios meet **WCAG AA** (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text). The lighter accents (Bright Forest, Gray Sky, Sunset Yellow, Sand) will require dark backgrounds to meet contrast minimums.
