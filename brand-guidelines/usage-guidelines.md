# TRPL Brand Usage Guidelines

## Logo Usage

### Do
- Use the approved logo files from `/logos/` — never redraw or recreate the logo
- Maintain minimum clear space around the logo: **2× the width of the crossbar of the "T" in "Theodore"** on all sides
- Use the correct color version for the background: full-color on light backgrounds, reversed (white) on dark backgrounds, single-color on special applications
- Scale the logo proportionally — always lock aspect ratio

### Don't
- Do not stretch, condense, rotate, or distort the logo
- Do not apply drop shadows, outlines, or effects to the logo
- Do not place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a clear zone or overlay treatment
- Do not use the wordmark and monogram simultaneously in the same composition unless using an approved lockup
- Do not reproduce the wordmark below **0.75" height** (minimum recommended per brand guidelines)
- Do not alter colors — use only approved logo color variants

---

## Color Usage

- Never introduce colors outside the approved palette without brand team approval
- Do not use the primary brand color as a substitute for black in body text
- Accent colors (Medora Blue, Prairie Gold) are accents — they should not dominate a composition
- For print applications, always use CMYK values; for digital, use RGB/HEX
- Ensure all text/background color combinations meet WCAG AA accessibility standards

---

## Typography Usage

- Do not set body copy smaller than 9pt (print) or 14px (web)
- Do not use decorative or script fonts outside of approved brand typefaces
- Do not use all-caps for body copy or long text blocks
- ITC Clearface is the body copy standard — do not substitute a similar-looking font without approval

---

## Photography & Imagery

See [`/photography/style-guide.md`](../photography/style-guide.md) for full photography direction. Key rules:

- Prioritize authentic, unposed imagery of the Badlands landscape and real people
- Avoid stock photography that could belong to any institution
- Do not use heavily filtered or stylized photography that conflicts with the brand's earnest, grounded tone
- Historical TR photography should be sourced from approved archives (Library of Congress, Theodore Roosevelt Collection at Harvard) and credited properly

---

## Dos and Don'ts: Writing

### Do
- Lead every piece of content with a specific, concrete story or detail
- Use TR's words verbatim with source attribution when quoting him
- Drive all content toward trlibrary.com/visit or relevant ticket/CTA links
- Use institutional terminology: participants, Trustees, benefactors, the Library

### Don't
- Do not paraphrase TR's words and present them as a direct quote
- Do not use "Teddy" in prose (hashtag exception only)
- Do not position TR's legacy as belonging to one political side
- Do not use vague aspirational language ("world-class," "transformative journey")
- Do not publish unverified gallery counts, energy reduction percentages, or sustainability claims without staff verification (see brand-overview.md for open items)

---

## Partner & Co-Branding

- TRPL logo always appears first (or at equal prominence) in co-branded materials
- Partner logos must be approved before use in any TRPL publication
- The 23 Crossings Foundation has a distinct brand voice — do not conflate it with TRPL in materials
- TRPLF (Foundation) and TRPL (Library) are separate entities; make clear which is being referenced
